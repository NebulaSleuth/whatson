Why What's On

Stop browsing.
Start watching.

Your libraries live on different servers and your shows live on different services. What's On TV puts tonight's answer on one screen.

01

One home for everything

Plex, Jellyfin, Emby and over-the-air live TV, blended into a single "what should I watch tonight" experience.

02

Watch from anywhere

Secure, encrypted remote access to your own server — your movies, shows and live channels on the road, over your own connection.

03

Every screen

Phone and tablet, Apple TV and Android TV, the web, and a Roku channel in the works — the same app, the same library.

04

Share with your people

Invite family and friends to your server with their own profile — no fuss, no exposing your network.

Where it runs

On the screen you're already holding.

What's On TV pairs with a small self-hosted server that talks to your media servers and tuners, then serves every screen in the house — and, with remote access, every screen outside it.
